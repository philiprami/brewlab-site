Brew Lab Support

Brew Lab is a coffee brewing journal for iPhone. Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Email info@raizatech.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version. We reply within a few business days.

Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my Brew Lab Pro subscription?

Open the paywall in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Brew Lab.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. Brew Lab has no accounts or servers. Deleting the app removes its data, so export a CSV first if you want a copy.

How do I export my brews?

Use the CSV export in the app. The file is created on your device and goes only where you choose to share it.

How do I delete my data?

Delete all brews and beans from Settings in the app, or delete the app.
