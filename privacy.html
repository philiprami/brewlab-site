Brew Lab Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2026

Brew Lab ("the app") is a coffee brewing journal for iPhone. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: Brew Lab does not collect any personal data.

Data we collect

None. Brew Lab has no accounts, no servers and no sign-in. We (the developer) never receive your brews, beans, ratings, notes or any other information you enter.

Where your data is stored

Everything you log in Brew Lab (brews, beans, ratings, notes and settings) is stored only on your device. It is not uploaded to us and is not synced to any cloud service by the app.

Analytics, tracking and third parties

Brew Lab contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. The app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and no data is shared with or sold to third parties.

Purchases

Brew Lab Pro is an optional subscription sold through the Apple App Store. Payments, subscription management and restores are handled entirely by Apple. We do not receive your payment details or Apple ID; the app only checks with Apple whether your subscription is active. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

CSV export

Brew Lab can export your brews as a CSV file. The file is created on your device and only leaves it if you choose to share it (for example via AirDrop, Mail or Files). Where it goes after that is up to you and the service you pick.

Deleting your data

You can delete all brews and beans from Settings in the app. Deleting the app from your iPhone removes all of its data. Data may also be included in your own device backups (e.g. iCloud or computer backups), which are controlled by you and Apple.

Children

Brew Lab is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
